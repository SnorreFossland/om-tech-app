## Introduction

Welcome to the Property Sales Platform! This web application connects property buyers and sellers through a streamlined process enhanced by AI-powered inquiry management. Whether you're browsing for your dream home, listing a property for sale, or managing inquiries as a platform representative, this guide will help you get started.

### Key Features

- **Browse Properties**: Search and filter property listings with detailed information
- **Smart Inquiries**: Submit inquiries that are processed by AI to generate professional responses
- **Save Favorites**: Keep track of properties you're interested in
- **List Properties**: Create and manage property listings as a seller
- **AI-Assisted Communication**: Representatives review AI-generated email drafts for efficient inquiry handling

---

## User Roles

The platform supports three distinct user roles:

### Buyer

- Browse and search property listings
- View detailed property information with photos
- Submit inquiries about properties
- Save properties to favorites
- Receive responses from property owners via representatives

### Seller

- Create and manage property listings
- Upload property photos (up to 20 images)
- Edit listing details and pricing
- Receive inquiry notifications via email
- Mark properties as sold or pending

### Representative

- Review incoming property inquiries
- Review and approve AI-generated email drafts
- Edit or regenerate email responses
- Forward approved messages to property owners
- Track inquiry status and communication history

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the **Sign Up** page
2. Enter your email address, name, and password
3. Select your account type:
   - **Buyer**: If you're looking to purchase property
   - **Seller**: If you're listing property for sale
   - **Representative**: (Admin-assigned only)
4. Click **Create Account**
5. Verify your email address (check your inbox for verification link)

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the **Login** page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You'll be redirected to the appropriate dashboard for your role

## Buyer Guide

### Browsing Properties

**Homepage Access**: No account required to browse listings

1. Visit the homepage to see featured properties
2. Use the **Search Bar** to find properties by city or keywords
3. Apply **Filters** to narrow your search:
   - **Property Type**: House, Apartment, Condo, Townhouse
   - **Price Range**: Set minimum and maximum budget
   - **Bedrooms**: Number of bedrooms needed
   - **Bathrooms**: Number of bathrooms needed
   - **Square Footage**: Minimum/maximum size
   - **Status**: Active, Sold, Pending (sold properties have "SOLD" badge)
4. Click on any property card to view full details

### Viewing Property Details

On the property detail page, you'll find:

- **Photo Gallery**: Swipe through property images
- **Property Information**: Price, size, bedrooms, bathrooms, type
- **Description**: Detailed property description
- **Amenities**: List of included features (e.g., parking, pool, garden)
- **Location**: Full address with map view
- **Contact Form**: Submit an inquiry directly

### Submitting an Inquiry

1. On the property detail page, locate the **Contact Form**
2. Fill in your information:
   - **Name**: Your full name
   - **Email**: Your contact email (will receive responses here)
   - **Phone**: (Optional) Your phone number
   - **Message**: Your inquiry or questions (minimum 10 characters)
3. Click **Submit Inquiry**
4. You'll see a confirmation: *"Your inquiry has been submitted and will be reviewed by our team"*
5. A platform representative will review your inquiry and forward it to the property owner
6. Expect a response within 24-48 hours via email

**What Happens Next?**

- Your inquiry is automatically processed by our AI system
- A platform representative receives a suggested email draft
- The representative reviews and approves the message
- The approved message is forwarded to the property owner
- The owner's response is sent directly to your email

### Managing Favorites

**Save Properties for Later**

1. Log in to your account
2. Browse properties and click the **Heart Icon** (♡) on any property card
3. The icon fills (♥) to indicate the property is saved
4. Access all your favorites from the **Favorites** page in your account menu

**Remove Favorites**

1. Go to your **Favorites** page
2. Click the filled heart icon (♥) on any property
3. The property is removed from your favorites list

### Viewing Sold Properties

- Sold properties display a **"SOLD"** badge on the listing card
- You can still view sold property details to see market pricing
- Sold properties are automatically archived after 30 days
- Archived properties no longer appear in search results

## Seller Guide

### Creating a Property Listing

1. Log in to your **Seller Account**
2. Navigate to **My Listings** or **Dashboard**
3. Click **Add New Property**
4. Fill in the required information:
   - **Title**: Catchy property headline (5-200 characters)
   - **Address**: Street address
   - **City, State, Zip Code**: Location details
   - **Property Type**: Select from House, Apartment, Condo, or Townhouse
   - **Price**: Listing price (USD)
   - **Bedrooms**: Number of bedrooms
   - **Bathrooms**: Number of bathrooms
   - **Square Footage**: Total living area
   - **Description**: Detailed property description (minimum 50 characters)
   - **Amenities**: Select applicable features (e.g., parking, pool, dishwasher)
5. Click **Save Draft** or **Publish**

### Uploading Property Photos

**Best Practices**: Use high-quality images showing different angles of your property

1. After creating your listing, click **Upload Photos**
2. Click **Choose Files** or drag-and-drop images
3. You can upload up to **20 images** (max 10MB each)
4. Supported formats: JPG, PNG
5. Arrange photo order by dragging thumbnails
6. The first photo will be the **primary photo** shown in search results
7. Click **Save Photo Order**

**Photo Tips**:

- Include exterior shots, living areas, bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms
- Use natural lighting when possible
- Show unique features (fireplace, view, renovations)
- Avoid cluttered or dark photos

### Managing Your Listings

**Edit a Listing**

1. Go to **My Listings**
2. Click **Edit** on any property
3. Update any field (title, price, description, amenities)
4. Click **Save Changes**

**Change Listing Status**

- **Active**: Property is available for sale (default)
- **Pending**: Property has an accepted offer
- **Sold**: Property sale is complete
- **Archived**: Remove from public view (can be restored)

To change status:

1. Open the property listing
2. Select new status from **Status** dropdown
3. Click **Update Status**
4. If marking as **Sold**, enter the sale date

### Receiving Inquiries

**Email Notifications**

When a buyer submits an inquiry about your property:

1. You'll receive an **email notification** to your registered email
2. The email includes:
   - Buyer's name and contact information
   - Their inquiry message
   - Property details
   - Link to respond

**Responding to Inquiries**

- **Option 1**: Reply directly to the email (message goes to the buyer)
- **Option 2**: Use the platform's built-in messaging (future feature)

**Important**: All initial inquiries are reviewed by platform representatives to ensure quality and filter spam.

### Deleting a Listing

1. Go to **My Listings**
2. Click **Delete** on the property you want to remove
3. Confirm deletion in the popup
4. **Warning**: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
5. Alternative: Use **Archived** status to hide without deleting

## Representative Guide

Representatives manage the inquiry workflow and review AI-generated email drafts before they're sent to property owners.

### Accessing the Representative Dashboard

1. Log in with your **Representative Account**
2. You'll see the **Representative Dashboard** with:
   - **Pending Inquiries**: New inquiries awaiting review
   - **Draft Ready**: Inquiries with AI-generated emails ready for approval
   - **In Progress**: Approved inquiries waiting for owner response
   - **Statistics**: Total inquiries, response times, approval rates

### Reviewing an Inquiry

1. From the dashboard, click on any inquiry in the **Draft Ready** section
2. You'll see:
   - **Inquiry Details**: Buyer's name, email, phone, message
   - **Property Information**: Title, address, price, owner details
   - **AI-Generated Email Draft**: Suggested message to send to the property owner

### AI Email Draft Review Process

The AI system automatically generates a professional email based on the buyer's inquiry. Your job is to review and approve (or edit) this draft.

**Email Draft Components**:

- **Subject Line**: Clearly identifies the property and inquiry type
- **Greeting**: Addresses the property owner by name
- **Context**: Summarizes the property details
- **Buyer Information**: Presents the buyer's inquiry professionally
- **Call to Action**: Requests the owner to respond
- **Signature**: Platform representative signature

**Review Checklist**:

- ✅ Greeting uses correct owner name
- ✅ Property address is accurate
- ✅ Buyer's inquiry is summarized appropriately
- ✅ Tone is professional and courteous
- ✅ Contact information is correct
- ✅ No spelling or grammar errors
- ✅ Language matches the buyer's inquiry language (AI auto-detects)

### Approving an Email Draft

**If the draft looks good:**

1. Review the AI-generated email
2. Scroll to the bottom of the draft preview
3. Click **Approve & Send**
4. Confirmation message appears: *"Email sent successfully to property owner"*
5. The inquiry status changes to **CONTACTED_OWNER**
6. The property owner receives the email immediately via Resend

**The email is sent from**: `noreply@propertyplatform.com`  
**Reply-to address**: Buyer's email (so owner can respond directly)

### Editing an Email Draft

**If the draft needs minor changes:**

1. Click **Edit Draft**
2. The email content becomes editable
3. Make your changes directly in the text editor
4. Click **Save & Send**
5. Your edited version is sent instead of the AI draft
6. The system tracks that you made manual edits

**Common Edits**:

- Adjusting tone (more formal/casual)
- Adding specific property details
- Clarifying buyer questions
- Correcting any AI misinterpretations

### Rejecting an Email Draft

**If the draft is unsuitable:**

1. Click **Reject Draft**
2. (Optional) Enter a reason for rejection in the popup
3. Click **Confirm Rejection**
4. The inquiry status returns to **PENDING_AI_PROCESSING**
5. You can request a new draft

**When to Reject**:

- Draft is inappropriate or unprofessional
- AI misunderstood the buyer's inquiry
- Property information is incorrect
- Language detection was wrong

### Regenerating an Email Draft

**If you want a completely new version:**

1. Click **Regenerate Draft**
2. (Optional) Provide specific instructions:
   - *"Make it more formal"*
   - *"Focus on the buyer's budget concerns"*
   - *"Emphasize urgency"*
3. Click **Generate New Draft**
4. AI creates a new draft based on your instructions
5. Review the new draft and approve, edit, or reject

**Regeneration Limit**: Up to 3 regenerations per inquiry (to prevent abuse)

### Tracking Inquiry Status

**Inquiry Lifecycle**:

1. **PENDING_AI_PROCESSING**: Just submitted, AI is generating draft
2. **DRAFT_READY**: AI draft is ready for your review
3. **APPROVED**: You approved the draft
4. **CONTACTED_OWNER**: Email sent to property owner
5. **RESPONDED_TO_BUYER**: Owner responded (tracked via email)
6. **CLOSED**: Inquiry resolved or buyer no longer interested
7. **REJECTED**: Draft was rejected (returns to step 1)

**Filtering Inquiries**:

- Use the **Status Filter** dropdown to view specific inquiry types
- Use the **Property Filter** to see inquiries for a specific listing
- Use the **Date Range** filter to view inquiries from specific time periods

### Representative Best Practices

- **Respond Quickly**: Aim to review and approve drafts within 4 hours during business hours
- **Personalize When Needed**: Don't be afraid to edit drafts to add a personal touch
- **Quality Over Speed**: Better to edit a draft than send something imperfect
- **Track Patterns**: If you're frequently rejecting drafts, provide feedback to improve the AI
- **Owner Context**: Consider the property owner's previous interactions when reviewing

### Viewing AI Performance Metrics

1. Go to **Settings** > **AI Performance**
2. View statistics:
   - **Approval Rate**: Percentage of drafts approved without edits
   - **Average Review Time**: How long it takes to review drafts
   - **Regeneration Rate**: How often drafts are regenerated
   - **Language Accuracy**: How often language detection is correct

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is the platform free to use?**  
A: Yes, browsing properties and submitting inquiries is completely free for buyers. Sellers can create listings for free (pricing details TBD).

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

**Q: Can I have both buyer and seller accounts?**  
A: No, each email can only be registered once. However, you can contact support to change your account type.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**  
A: The platform works on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). Mobile browsers are also supported.

### Buyer Questions

**Q: How long does it take to get a response to my inquiry?**  
A: Most inquiries are reviewed by representatives within 4 hours and forwarded to owners within 24 hours. Owner response times vary.

**Q: Can I inquire about multiple properties at once?**  
A: Yes, you can submit separate inquiries for as many properties as you'd like.

**Q: Why do inquiries go through representatives instead of directly to owners?**  
A: Representatives ensure inquiry quality, filter spam, and provide professional communication, improving the experience for both buyers and sellers.

**Q: Can I see sold properties?**  
A: Yes, sold properties are visible with a "SOLD" badge for 30 days before being auto-archived. This helps you understand market pricing.

**Q: Are my favorites visible to others?**  
A: No, your favorites list is private and only visible to you when logged in.

### Seller Questions

**Q: How do I know if someone is interested in my property?**  
A: You'll receive an email notification whenever a buyer submits an inquiry about your property.

**Q: Can I edit my listing after publishing?**  
A: Yes, you can edit any aspect of your listing at any time from the "My Listings" page.

**Q: What happens when my property sells?**  
A: Change the status to "SOLD" in your listing settings. The property will display a "SOLD" badge and auto-archive after 30 days.

**Q: Can I temporarily hide my listing?**  
A: Yes, change the status to "ARCHIVED" to remove it from public view. You can restore it anytime.

**Q: What image formats are supported?**  
A: JPG and PNG formats are supported, up to 10MB per image.

**Q: How many photos can I upload?**  
A: You can upload up to 20 photos per property listing.

### Representative Questions

**Q: What languages does the AI support?**  
A: The AI automatically detects the inquiry language and generates responses in the same language (supports 50+ languages via GPT-4o).

**Q: How accurate are the AI-generated drafts?**  
A: The AI has a high approval rate (tracked in your dashboard). You should always review drafts, but most require minimal or no edits.

**Q: Can I provide feedback on AI draft quality?**  
A: Yes, use the rejection reason field to provide feedback, which helps improve future drafts.

**Q: What if the AI misunderstands an inquiry?**  
A: You can either edit the draft directly or use the "Regenerate" feature with specific instructions to guide the AI.

**Q: How do I handle urgent inquiries?**  
A: Filter inquiries by date to prioritize recent submissions. You can also add "Urgent" in your edited drafts when regenerating.
